# Metric Tracker design system

## Visual world

**Research Desk** is a quiet, editorial operations system for an in-house marketing team. It treats information as something to inspect closely: near-white paper, graphite type, fine structural rules, and violet used as the active thread through a field of otherwise restrained information.

## Foundations

- **Primary signal:** `#8E6AFA`; it marks the selected, actionable, or changing thing—not general decoration.
- **Surfaces:** warm paper backgrounds (`#F7F6F3`), a lifted white sheet (`#FFFFFF`), and deep ink (`#1E1D22`).
- **Supporting ink:** muted graphite (`#6C6A72`) for contextual reading; cool lavender tints are reserved for active surface states.
- **Type:** a high-character display serif gives editorial authority; a compact sans-serif handles UI and data. Numbers use tabular figures.
- **Structure:** 12px radius, 1px rules in soft stone, spacious page margins, and asymmetrical editorial layouts rather than a field of equal cards.

## Components

- **Wordmark:** compact violet square mark plus the product name; use the mark alone in constrained spaces.
- **Primary action:** solid violet button with white text, slight vertical lift on hover, and a strong visible focus ring.
- **Sheets:** white panels with a 1px rule and 12px radius. Avoid stacked cards and floating decorative shadows.
- **Metric states:** neutral at rest; violet for focus/current selection; green for favorable movement; amber for attention; red only for harmful states.
- **Navigation:** dense, quiet labels with the current item marked by a violet inset rule or violet text—not a heavy filled nav pill.

## Interaction

Use short, deliberate transitions. Surface entry is one orchestrated reveal; routine hover and focus states stay quick and functional. Honor reduced-motion preferences.

## Responsive behavior

Preserve reading priority over symmetry: side material moves below the main action on small screens, page padding contracts, and controls remain at least 44px tall.
